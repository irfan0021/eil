Overview

Incorporated in March 2025, EIL Arms and Ammunition Limited (EAAL) is a premier defence manufacturing firm dedicated to strengthening India’s strategic autonomy. As a powerful subsidiary of infrastructure giant Eagle Infra India Limited and Inderdeep Infra India Limited, we bridge the gap between world-class engineering and advanced defence production.

Strategic Partnership with the Indian Army

In a landmark commitment to national security, EIL has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Indian Army. Under the Aatmanirbhar Bharat (Self-Reliant India) initiative, EIL stands as a trusted strategic partner ready to deliver critical defence requirements as and when required, ensuring the nation’s armed forces are equipped with indigenous, high-performance technology.

Manufacturing Powerhouse: The Hyderabad Hub

We are currently developing a state-of-the-art defence ecosystem on 35 acres of land in Jedcherla, near Hyderabad, Telangana. This facility serves as our primary engine for:

  • Small Arms & Ammunition: Precision manufacturing of small, medium and high-calibre defence hardware and ballistics.
  • UAV Excellence Center: A dedicated wing for the manufacturing, development and testing of next-generation Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs).

Vision & Mission

Vision:
To become a leading provider of high-quality, indigenous defense solutions that enhance national security and self-reliance in the arms and ammunition sector.

Mission:

  • Innovation: Developing advanced metal fabrication techniques for precision-engineered ammunition.
  • Quality & Safety: Adhering to stringent international standards for defense manufacturing and safety.
  • National Contribution: Supporting the "Make in India" initiative by providing domestic alternatives to imported defense equipment.
  • Stakeholder Value: Delivering excellence and reliability to defense forces and strategic partners through cost-effective and innovative solutions.
