Unmanned Aerial Systems

Eye in the Sky, Grip on the Ground

At EIL Defence, we are committed to developing next-generation Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) that enhance operational superiority across modern battlefields and complex mission environments.

Our indigenous UAV platforms are engineered to deliver intelligence, precision, endurance, and reliability, supporting the evolving requirements of defence, homeland security, and strategic operations.

Our portfolio encompasses Loitering Munition UAVs, Surveillance & Reconnaissance (ISR) Platforms, and Logistics Drones, each designed to operate effectively in high-altitude regions, mountainous terrain, deserts, dense forests, coastal environments, and other challenging operational theatres.

Built with advanced autonomous flight control systems, these platforms provide long-endurance missions, real-time situational awareness, precision navigation, and dependable performance under demanding conditions.

From persistent intelligence gathering and target acquisition to critical payload delivery and tactical mission support, EIL Defence UAVs are designed to meet the operational needs of modern armed forces. By integrating cutting-edge technologies in autonomous navigation, secure communications, artificial intelligence, and mission management, our aerial systems deliver scalable, mission-ready solutions that strengthen national security and operational effectiveness.

Multicopters

Dual Hexa 35.5

Designed with a dual coaxial hexa-rotor architecture, the Dual Hexa 35.5 provides superior lifting capability and long-endurance performance. Built for demanding autonomous missions, it carries heavy payloads with precision while operating on EIL's intelligent autonomous flight control platform, ensuring stability, safety, and mission success.

Quad 4.1

The Quad 4.1 is an entry-level autonomous UAV designed for speed, agility, and operational efficiency. Its lightweight construction enables rapid deployment, while its compact design allows seamless navigation through dense vegetation and restricted terrain. Ideal for mapping, inspection, and reconnaissance missions, it is equipped with EIL's advanced autonomous flight control system, ensuring precision, stability, and reliable mission execution.

Endurance30–60 min
Max Speed10 m/s

Hexa 15.5

The Hexa 15.5 is a versatile six-rotor autonomous UAV engineered to deliver enhanced lifting capability, flight stability, and operational redundancy. Designed for missions requiring greater payload capacity than a quadcopter while maintaining excellent range and endurance, it is ideal for surveillance, inspection, mapping, and industrial applications. Powered by EIL's advanced autonomous flight control system, it ensures reliable, precise, and efficient mission execution.

Range10 km
Endurance25–40 min
Max Speed10 m/s

Octa 60.20

The Octa 60.20 is EIL's flagship heavy-lift autonomous UAV, engineered to deliver exceptional payload capacity, endurance, and operational reliability. Featuring an eight-rotor configuration and a maximum take-off weight of 56 kg, it is purpose-built for demanding missions such as precision agriculture, bulk material distribution, logistics, and industrial operations. Powered by EIL's advanced autonomous flight control system, it ensures precise navigation, superior stability, and dependable performance in challenging environments.

Range20 km
Endurance20–40 min
Max Speed15 m/s
