About EIL Defence

Building India's Next Generation of Defence Manufacturing

EIL Arms And Ammunition Ltd was incorporated in 2025. Our Small Caliber Arms & Ammunition Manufacturing Facility in Vattem Village, Nagarkurnool District, Telangana is spread over more than 40 Acres with in-house test facility and range.

We also aim to establish Barrel manufacturing and rifling indigenously.

Precision Inspection at High Speed

EIL Arms And Ammunition Ltd has the world's fastest and most accurate Arms & Ammunition inspection system available.

It is a high speed comprehensive inspection system that detects all critical defects according to MIL-STD-636 for small calibre, and MIL-STD-651 for medium calibre ammunition and components.

Loitering Munition

A tactical UAV concept designed for extended loiter, surveillance, target identification and rapid response.

  • Designed for long loiter time over target areas.
  • Real-time surveillance and reconnaissance.
  • Can be redirected or aborted mid-mission.
  • Electro-optical/infrared sensors for target identification.
  • Integrates into wider command-and-control networks.
  • Precision strike capability.
  • Different autonomy levels with human oversight requirements.
  • Compact and portable for tactical deployment.
  • Rapid response against time-sensitive targets.
  • Balances range, endurance and payload for mission role.
